Before you start

A few useful
answers.

Is Sportspro available for iPhone?

This download is an Android APK. It cannot be installed on iOS.

How do I get premium access?

Contact the team on Telegram for current plans, pricing and access instructions. The current app uses a shared password.

Why does a channel sometimes buffer?

Playback depends on your connection and the stream provider. A slow or unavailable source can cause buffering or prevent playback.

Does every video support HD or 4K?

No. Resolution depends on the source stream, your device and available bandwidth. Sportspro does not guarantee a particular quality.

Where can I find announcements?

Open Latest updates inside the app, or visit the Telegram channel for information from the developer.

What should I do if installation fails?

Check available storage and confirm that the APK downloaded completely. For an update, use an APK signed by the same developer. Contact support before uninstalling if you need to preserve app data.

What if the download does not start?

Open this website in Chrome and try again. You can also download the attached APK from the GitHub release page using View release details above.
